SIMulacrum

(a locally run WoW gear sim)

Your character's simulated twin. Paste your SimulationCraft export, pick what to try, and see what does the most damage.

Checking SimulationCraft…
Download

1 Paste your character

  1. Install the SimulationCraft addon from CurseForge or Wago, then log in to your character.
  2. Type /simc in chat and press Enter. A window full of text opens.
  3. Copy all of it: click inside the window, press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
  4. Paste it in the box below with Ctrl+V.

What to sim

Tick everything you want. They run one after another when you click Run, and each opens in its own tab on the results page.

2 Gear to try

Equipped items are always included. Rings and trinkets are tried in every valid pair.

3 Upgrades & Catalyst optional

Item upgrade currency

Adds an upgraded copy of every equipped and ticked item, at the highest rank your crests cover. Ranks at or below your best item level for that slot are free.

Catalyst charges

The most catalyst items allowed in one combination.

Adds a tier-set version of every equipped and ticked item the catalyst can convert.

4 Gems optional

Diamonds get stronger for each different gem color you have socketed.

5 Food, flask, potion & oil optional

"Used in every sim" applies to all results. Tick others to compare them against it.

6 Talent loadouts optional

Each ticked loadout is simmed with your current gear.

7 Fight settings

SimulationCraft engine

After a WoW patch or hotfix, update the engine so it knows the latest items and tuning.

Build it from source instead

Open PowerShell, paste this, and press Enter. It takes a few minutes; don't start a sim while it runs.
