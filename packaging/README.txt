GEAR SIM
Find out which gear, gems, food and flasks do the most damage for your
World of Warcraft character, using SimulationCraft. Runs on your own PC.

STARTING IT
1. Double-click GearSim.exe. Your browser opens to the Gear Sim page.
2. Keep the black window open while you use it. Close it to quit.

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then
"Run anyway". This shows up because the app isn't code-signed.

SIMMING YOUR CHARACTER
1. Install the "SimulationCraft" addon (CurseForge or Wago).
2. In game, type /simc and press Enter.
3. In the window that opens, press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C to copy.
4. Paste it into the Gear Sim page, tick what you want to try, and click Run sim.

UPDATES
After a WoW patch, click "Check for updates" at the bottom of the page.

More info: https://github.com/ktmsft/wow-gear-sim
